CognaAssist · Website chatbot

CognaAssist

Every question a visitor brings to your website, answered in the moment they ask it.

Most visitors leave a website without ever filling in a form. CognaAssist sits on your pages, answers from your own content, and turns a passing question into a conversation your team can follow up.

Keep scrolling. Each idea below gathers the stars into a shape. Drag a shape to turn it.

It answers like your best front-desk person

CognaAssist is trained on your website, brochures and FAQs, so it answers questions about your products, pricing and processes in your own words. When a question needs a person, it says so and hands over cleanly.

It keeps to what your business has published. Visitors get a straight answer or a route to your team, never a guess.

It lives on the site you already have

There is nothing to rebuild. CognaAssist is added to your existing website with a single snippet, takes on your colours and tone, and works on phones as well as desktops.

Your team decides what it can talk about and what it should hand over, and can change that at any time.

Conversations become leads

When a visitor is ready to talk, CognaAssist collects their name and requirement and passes it straight to your team, with the whole conversation attached so nobody has to ask the same question twice.

Want to see it on your own website? Cognavia can set up a working demo trained on your pages.
